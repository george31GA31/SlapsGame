A tournament starts with eight ready players. A league starts with 2–20 ready players; everyone plays each other once.

Online league tiebreaks: points, then games (rounds) won minus lost, games won, slaps won minus lost, then slaps won. Exact ties share a position. Win: 1 point; loss: 0. These online regulations differ from the combined formula in the original Law I.3.5.
